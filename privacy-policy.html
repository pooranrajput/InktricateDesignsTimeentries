Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Inktricate Designs Time Tracker ("we," "our," or "us") collects the following information:

  • Employee Information: Names, email addresses, hourly rates, and employment status
  • Time Entry Data: Work hours, task categories, dates, and project descriptions
  • QuickBooks Data: Vendor information, bill details, and accounting data as necessary for payroll processing
  • Authentication Data: Login credentials and session information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Track employee work hours and calculate payroll
  • Generate contractor bills in QuickBooks
  • Provide administrative reporting and analytics
  • Ensure accurate 1099 tax reporting for contractors
  • Maintain system security and user authentication

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties except:

  • QuickBooks Integration: Payroll and vendor data is shared with QuickBooks for billing purposes
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Service Providers: Trusted partners who assist in operating our platform

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Secure database storage with encryption
  • Session-based authentication
  • Role-based access controls
  • Regular security updates and monitoring

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our agreements

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

7. QuickBooks Integration

Our integration with QuickBooks Online:

  • Accesses your QuickBooks data only for payroll and billing purposes
  • Creates vendors and bills as authorized by your administrator
  • Follows QuickBooks' security and privacy standards
  • Can be disconnected at any time through your QuickBooks account settings
